# OpportunityAI Platform — Architecture

## Purpose
OpportunityAI is a modular AI platform designed to detect, score and recommend real opportunities across multiple verticals.

## Current Vertical
- Real Estate: CostaBlancaFinder AI

## Future Verticals
- Jobs
- Flights
- Investments
- Business Opportunities

## Core Architecture
Data Sources → Ingestion Engine → Normalization → Scoring Engine → Database → Dashboards → Agents

## Shared Engines
- shared/scoring
- shared/database
- shared/geo
- shared/recommendations
- shared/monitoring

## Strategy
CostaBlancaFinder AI remains the first operational product while OpportunityAI becomes the global reusable platform.
